# Privacy policy: Tab Labeler

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Tab Labeler does not collect, send, sell or share any data.

- **What it reads:** the title and web address of each page you open, so it can make a short tab label.
- **Where that goes:** nowhere. It stays inside your browser. The extension has no servers, no analytics and no tracking.
- **What it saves:** your settings and custom labels (in Chrome's sync storage, so they follow your Chrome profile), and any labels you choose to report with the "Report it" button (on this computer only). You can clear reports from the popup, and removing the extension deletes everything.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/warrenchongdv/tab-labeler/issues
